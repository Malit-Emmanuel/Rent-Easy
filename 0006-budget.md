# ADR-0006 Planning budget and unit-cost target

Status: Accepted as planning assumption | Date: 2026-09-30

## Decision
12-month envelope KES 8-15M, released in stages by milestone. Verification target <= KES 500 per successfully verified unit, to be driven down with volume.

## Consequences / open items
Both numbers are unvalidated. The unit-cost target must be tested in the manual trial on 30-50 units before it becomes a ceiling. See build plan risk notes.
